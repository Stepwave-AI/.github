## About us

Stepwave delivers a novel brain-body health model that gives you the knowledge, community and structured support to strengthen your resilience. We combine expert-guided brain-body exercise with personalized digital training at home, turning brain health into a playful daily habit.

Our approach is based on the **Brain-IT** concept, developed and tested through six years of research.

## What makes us different

- **Play with your heart, not just your feet**: games that blend moving, thinking and breathing to strengthen the brain-heart connection.
- **Personalized to your potential**: games adapt to you and help build your cognitive reserve.
- **Together we move mountains**: a supportive community that makes training a long-term habit.
- **Developed with you, for you**: built with researchers, users, clinicians and game designers.

## Your weekly rhythm

| Cadence | What happens |

| Most days | 15 to 25 minutes of home training on your TV |
